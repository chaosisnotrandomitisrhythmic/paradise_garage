# Eternal Sun 2026 — Dusk-to-Dawn Listening Guide

A sunset→sunrise arc built in the spirit of the **Eternal Sun** festival (Sougia, Crete), grounded in the *actual selectors* on the bill — Optimo, Nosedrip/STROOM, Bufiman/Wolf Müller, OKO DJ/BFDM, Amelia Holt, Donna Leake, Tako & Orpheu the Wizard (Music From Memory / Red Light Radio), Rosie Ama, babyschön.

The playlist is just the running order — **the listening guide is the point.** Every record here was traced to a real tracklist, label page or shop listing that a research pass actually fetched. Nothing was pulled from Spotify's "sounds-like" engine; Spotify was treated as the *least* authoritative source, per the brief.

> **Verification & honesty notes**
>
> - **Never invented:** no fabricated tracks, links, prices, or tracklists. Where a page couldn't be loaded, the entry says so and gives a *search path*, not a made-up URL.
> - **Link confidence:** ✅ = page fetched & confirmed · 🔎 = titled search match, page not opened (treat as ~95%, spot-check) · ❓ = lead only, verify before trusting.
> - **Discogs** blocks automated fetching, so Discogs links are given as **search paths** (real, functional) unless a specific release ID surfaced in a snippet — those are marked 🔎.
> - **BPM / key:** not reliably found in any verified source. Rather than trust third-party BPM databases, this guide leaves them blank — **check against the file yourself.** ("not found" throughout.)
> - **Availability / prices** are a snapshot as of **2026-07-13** and move fast, especially sold-out vinyl.
> - **Selector attribution** ("via …") = the mix/show/label where the record was found. Tako & Orpheu keep their tracklists secret (confirmed in Orpheu's Stamp The Wax interview), so the **Music From Memory catalogue is used as the honest proxy** for their sound.

## 🌅 PHASE 1 — SUNSET / WARM LEFTFIELD-BALEARIC (people still in the water)

### 1. Joan Bibiloni – Sa Fosca
*El Sur* — **Music From Memory (MFM002)**, 2015 · beatless Balearic
- **Format:** vinyl **in stock** + digital. LP €27 (ships ~2 days), digital €13.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://music-from-memory.bandcamp.com/album/el-sur) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Joan Bibiloni El Sur MFM"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Joan+Bibiloni+El+Sur+Music+From+Memory&type=release)
- **Source:** MFM Bandcamp label page (track 9, 7:02).
- **Note:** The Mallorcan guitarist's long-form drift — tape loops, a soft drum computer, Mediterranean guitar — off the compilation that made MFM's name. A Balearic-island record for a Cretan beach is almost too on-the-nose, and that's exactly why it opens. Seven minutes that unfold rather than build; lay it while people are still swimming and mix out of the ambient tail into anything with a pulse.

### 2. Gaussian Curve – Impossible Island
*Clouds* — **Music From Memory (MFM004)**, 2015 · ambient/Balearic
- **Format:** digital €10; **LP sold out** at label → secondary market.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://music-from-memory.bandcamp.com/album/clouds) · SoundCloud 🔎 (label set, unfetched) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Gaussian Curve Clouds MFM"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Gaussian+Curve+Clouds&type=release)
- **Source:** MFM Bandcamp (track 2, 7:28).
- **Note:** Gigi Masin (Rhodes), Jonny Nash (guitar/trumpet) and Young Marco — the scene's in-house supergroup, cut live over one Amsterdam weekend. Warm chords, brushed guitar, no drums to fight. Beatless, so blend on texture; let the crowd's chatter be the percussion. Follows Bibiloni without a tempo — pure mood glue.

### 3. Horace Tapscott with the Pan-Afrikan Peoples Arkestra – Coltrane Medley
*Flight 17* — Nimbus West (orig. 1978); **Outernational Sounds** reissue, 2014 · spiritual jazz
- **Format:** vinyl (reissue LP) + digital.
- **Hear / buy:** [Outernational Sounds BC 🔎](https://outernationalsounds.bandcamp.com/album/flight-17) · [Nimbus West BC 🔎](https://nimbuswest.bandcamp.com/album/flight-17) · Discogs 🔎 [release/3693277](https://www.discogs.com/release/3693277)
- **Source:** **Donna Leake, NTS, 11 May 2026** (track 4). Recorded live in an LA church; this cut was previously CD-only.
- **Note:** A community-orchestra Coltrane devotional that breathes rather than swings — strings and horns swelling in church acoustics. Zero percussive urgency, maximum intent: the "the night is sacred" statement piece for the first hour. Play it nearly full-length and mix out of the applause-decay into hand drums.

### 4. Pablo's Eye – That Night Together With Her
*Spring Break* — **STROOM.tv**, 2018 · leftfield / spoken-word ambient
- **Format:** digital €12; **all 3 vinyl pressings sold out** → secondary only.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://stroomtv.bandcamp.com/album/spring-break) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Pablo's Eye Spring Break Stroom"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Pablos+Eye+Spring+Break+Stroom&type=release)
- **Source:** STROOM label Bandcamp — the archival trilogy that built the label.
- **Note:** Brussels collective led by Axel Libeert: whispered spoken word, sun-faded synths, field recordings — the sound of a half-remembered Mediterranean holiday. Beatless-to-loose-pulse, so float it over a long crossfade. This is where Optimo's Belgian world and MFM's Balearic one quietly touch.

### 5. Finis Africæ – El Secreto De Las 12 (The Secret Of 12 O'Clock)
EM Records reissue (EM1118LP), orig. 1980s Spain · organic ambient / exotica
- **Format:** vinyl (reissue LP) + CD; original-era vinyl rare.
- **Hear / buy:** [Forced Exposure 🔎](https://www.forcedexposure.com/Catalog/finis-africae-el-secreto-de-las-12-the-secret-of-12-o-clock-lp/EM.1118LP.html) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Finis Africae EM Records"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Finis+Africae+El+Secreto&type=release)
- **Source:** **BFDM w/ Tiff & OKO DJ, NTS, 17 Apr 2017** (fetched tracklist).
- **Note:** Spanish flutes, hand percussion and field recordings — a José Padilla / Café del Mar sunset staple long before the reissue. OKO's crew *opened an NTS show* with it, which tells you how they hear it. Its loose percussion bed hands straight over to the German tribal-drum material coming next — a near-seamless join.

## 🟠 PHASE 2 — GOLDEN HOUR / FIRST PULSE (sun on the horizon, bodies start moving)

### 6. Om Buschman – Hey Tata Gorem (Wolf Müller Edit)
*Tropical Drums of Deutschland* — **Music For Dreams**, 2017 · **official edit** · cosmic tribal
- **Format:** digital (99 DKK) + ltd CD; **2LP sold out** on Bandcamp.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://janschulte-musicfordreams.bandcamp.com/album/tropical-drums-of-deutschland) · Discogs 🔎 [master/1227799](https://www.discogs.com/master/1227799)
- **Source:** the comp itself, compiled by Jan Schulte. *(Label correction: Music For Dreams, not Music From Memory.)*
- **Note:** The thesis statement of the whole "Tropical Drums of Deutschland" aesthetic — late-80s German new-age percussion made floor-usable by Schulte's edit hand. The moment the beach crowd tips from listening into moving. The unedited original sits two tracks earlier on the comp if you want the looser version for deeper dusk.

### 7. Vito Ricci – The Ship Was Sailing
*I Was Crossing A Bridge* — **Music From Memory (MFM006)**, 2015 · dubbed electronic funk
- **Format:** digital €13; **2xLP sold out** → secondary.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://music-from-memory.bandcamp.com/album/i-was-crossing-a-bridge) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Vito Ricci I Was Crossing A Bridge"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Vito+Ricci+I+Was+Crossing+A+Bridge&type=release)
- **Source:** MFM Bandcamp (track 1, 5:43).
- **Note:** NYC downtown-scene outsider electronics, exhumed by MFM — hand percussion floating over drifting synths. The hinge between beach ambient and the first real dance record: the "permission to move" moment. ("Dub It" from the same comp is the sleazier backup.)

### 8. Cat Stevens – Was Dog A Doughnut
*Izitso* — Island, 1977 · proto-electro curveball
- **Format:** widely available album vinyl + streaming.
- **Hear / buy:** Discogs 🔎 [search "Cat Stevens Izitso"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Cat+Stevens+Izitso&type=release) · streaming (Tidal/Apple likely; Spotify not checked per brief)
- **Source:** **Optimo, NTS, 9 June 2026** (track 22).
- **Note:** The famous "wait — what IS this?" record: Cat Stevens, Chick Corea and a synth groove that predates electro by five years. A certified Balearic weapon. Drop it as the sun hits the water; its rubbery machine-funk lets you pivot from ambient into actual groove without a hard edit.

### 9. Mystic Jungle – Money Wonder
*Deviant Disco* — **Periodica Records**, 2022 (reissued) · modern italo-funk
- **Format:** digital; **both vinyl pressings sold out**.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://periodicarecords.bandcamp.com/album/deviant-disco-reissue) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Mystic Jungle Deviant Disco"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Mystic+Jungle+Deviant+Disco&type=release)
- **Source:** **babyschön, NTS, 8 Nov 2024** (track 7).
- **Note:** Naples' Periodica crew (Mystic Jungle / Whodamanny orbit) doing greasy italo-funk that sounds forty years old. Exactly where the sunset records end and dancing begins. Two sold-out pressings in two years — digital is the realistic play.

## 🥁 PHASE 3 — DEEP NIGHT / DUBBED COSMIC & TRIBAL DRUMS (the engine room)

### 10. Wolf Müller – Balztanz
*Balztanz EP* — **Themes For Great Cities (TFGC008)**, 2013 · Salon des Amateurs tribal
- **Format:** digital €6 (24-bit); original 12" on Discogs.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://janschultemusic.bandcamp.com/album/balztanz) · [TFGC SoundCloud 🔎](https://soundcloud.com/themes-for-great-cities/sets/tfgc008-wolf-m-ller-balztanz)
- **Source:** Wolf Müller's own Bandcamp ("drum music for peace"). 7:14.
- **Note:** Jaw harps (Maultrommeln), hand drums, forest psychedelia — *the* foundational Salon des Amateurs tribal-drums record. Long percussion intro/outro makes it trivially blendable at mid-tempo. "Dickicht" on the same EP is the bigger cult alternate.

### 11. Bim Sherman – Danger Dub
*Matrix Dub* / *Ghetto Dub* (orig. 1988) — **Week-End Records** reissue, 2026 · JA dub
- **Format:** vinyl (2026 reissue in print) + digital.
- **Hear / buy:** [Week-End BC 🔎](https://week-end.bandcamp.com/album/ghetto-dub) · Discogs 🔎 [master/808326](https://www.discogs.com/master/808326) · Boomkat 🔎 (listing exists)
- **Source:** **Optimo, NTS, 12 May 2026** (track 3).
- **Note:** Deep, spacious dub from the Bim Sherman vaults — dub as *cosmic engine*, the register Optimo use to reset a room without losing it. Huge low end at low tempo; ride the tail of its delays into a minimal-synth intro and the tempo jump reads as intentional.

### 12. The Mauskovic Dance Band – Drinks By The Sea
s/t — **Soundway Records**, 2019 · space-disco cumbia
- **Format:** digital + vinyl LP + streaming.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp 🔎](https://mauskovicdanceband.bandcamp.com/track/drinks-by-the-sea) · Soundway 🔎
- **Source:** **Optimo, NTS, 9 June 2026** (track 23).
- **Note:** Amsterdam's no-wave cumbia outfit — hypnotic Afro-Caribbean drum-machine chug, literally titled for the setting. Mid-evening motorik that grooves without demanding peak energy; takes dub sirens, percussion and acapellas like a champ.

### 13. Tapes Meets The Drums Of Wareika Hill Sounds – Datura Mystic
12" — **Honest Jon's Records**, 2015 · nyabinghi dub
- **Format:** vinyl 12" **£10.99 in stock** + digital £2+ (includes digital).
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅ (in stock)](https://honestjonsrecords.bandcamp.com/album/datura-mystic) · Discogs 🔎 [release/6896871](https://www.discogs.com/release/6896871) · also honestjons.com / Boomkat 🔎
- **Source:** **Donna Leake, NTS, 11 May 2026** (track 17).
- **Note:** A real Rastafari grounation drumming session on Wareika Hill, dubbed out by Jahtari's Tapes — "binghi dub." Hand drums + delay = the perfect tempo-ambiguous corridor around midnight; the pulse lets you land anywhere from 70 to 140 on the far side. B-side is a deeper dub version to stay in it longer. **One of the few confirmed in-stock buys — grab it.**

### 14. Bufiman – Peace Moves
*Peace Moves EP* — **Dekmantel (DKMNTL051)**, 2017 · dubbed drum science
- **Format:** vinyl 12" + digital.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp](https://bufiman.bandcamp.com/album/peace-moves) · [Dekmantel SoundCloud 🔎](https://soundcloud.com/dkmntl/dkmntl051-bufiman-peace-moves-ep)
- **Source:** Bufiman Bandcamp discography + Dekmantel/Bleep listings.
- **Note:** "Percussive soul" — broken, dubbed-out drums with that Düsseldorf wonk, the record that moved Schulte's Bufiman alias from edits into original Dekmantel territory. Three versions of the title cut (incl. a DJ Normal 4 remix) let you pick your energy.

### 15. Wolf Müller & Niklas Wandt – Welcome Zum Paradies
*Instrumentalmusik von der Mitte der World* — **Growing Bin (GBR013)**, 2018 · krautfusion / tribal house
- **Format:** digital €10; **2LP sold out** at BC → via growingbinrecords.com.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://wolfmuellerundniklaswandt.bandcamp.com/album/instrumentalmusik-von-der-mitte-der-world) · [Growing Bin 🔎](https://www.growingbinrecords.com/growing-bin-releases/gbr013/)
- **Source:** Bandcamp album page ("Westfalen Kraut Fusion / Tribal House"). 10:55.
- **Note:** Live-drummer krautfusion — Wandt's kit against Müller's percussion toys, "lucid-dream" material. At ~11 minutes it's a set-piece, not a transition tool: drop it around 2am when the floor trusts you. "Der Mitte Der World" (11:57) is an equally valid pull from the same 2LP.

### 16. Om – Cremation Ghat II
*God Is Good* — **Drag City**, 2009 · psych drone-groove
- **Format:** vinyl LP/CD + streaming.
- **Hear / buy:** [Drag City 🔎](https://www.dragcity.com/products/god-is-good) · streaming widely
- **Source:** **Donna Leake, NTS, 6 July 2026** (track 10 — between exotica and Muslimgauze).
- **Note:** Albini-recorded — Al Cisneros' meditative bass under tablas, strings and flute; stoner-doom lineage, but this cut is all Eastern drone-groove, no distortion. The 2am left turn that keeps heads nodding while resetting harmonic expectations before the machines arrive. Donna ran it into Muslimgauze — that Middle-Eastern-percussion throughline is your exit.

### 17. Wetman – Lagoon Dub
*The Wettening EP* — **Vivid Recordings (VIVR06)**, 2020 · dub interlude
- **Format:** vinyl 12" + digital.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp 🔎](https://vividrecordings.bandcamp.com/track/lagoon-dub) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Wetman The Wettening Vivid"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Wetman+Wettening&type=release)
- **Source:** **OKO DJ, NTS, 25 Feb 2022** (fetched tracklist).
- **Note:** Thick bass, articulate drums, dubbed-out sample haze — where the OKO/Marseille side shakes hands with Düsseldorf. Use it as the connective tissue from tribal-drums into the colder synth section: keeps the low-end conversation going while draining the sunshine out of the room.

## ⚡ PHASE 4 — PEAK / EBM · COLDWAVE · MINIMAL SYNTH · SLOW-TECHNO (2–4am)

### 18. Liaisons Dangereuses – Los Niños Del Parque
12" — 1981 · **proto-EBM anthem**
- **Format:** vinyl (many pressings/reissues) + streaming.
- **Hear / buy:** Discogs 🔎 [master/71073](https://www.discogs.com/master/71073) · appears on [How To Kill The DJ Pt2 🔎](https://www.discogs.com/release/323830)
- **Source:** **Optimo's own mix CD "How To Kill The DJ [Part Two]" (Tigersushi, 2004)**, pos 1.15.
- **Note:** Beate Bartel & Chrislo Haas: sequenced menace and Spanish vocal chants — baked into Optimo DNA (they cut it against Soft Cell's "Sex Dwarf" on the mix). Your detonator: the bassline alone locks a floor, and it slides into early Chicago house or DAF-school body music with zero friction.

### 19. Group Rhoda – Virtual Dancer
*Out of Time, Out of Touch* — **Night School (LSSN012)**, 2012 · minimal wave
- **Format:** vinyl LP + digital.
- **Hear / buy:** [Night School BC 🔎](https://night-school.bandcamp.com/track/virtual-dancer) · [YouTube 🔎](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IV1SseKUnRc)
- **Source:** **OKO DJ, NTS, 25 Feb 2022** — she played *two* Group Rhoda tracks (an endorsement, not a fluke).
- **Note:** Bass-heavy Bay Area minimal wave, a detached vocal about virtual contact — cold but body-moving, the OKO 3am register. Slot after the dub section when you want the mood nocturnal without a BPM jump. "Fire" from the same session is the harder alternate.

### 20. Nini Raviolette – Suis-Je Normale ?
12" — Celluloid, 1980; **Tigersushi** reissue, 2004 · French minimal-synth
- **Format:** vinyl + rips; some streaming.
- **Hear / buy:** Discogs 🔎 [release/222004](https://www.discogs.com/release/222004) — originals **from ~$80** · [YouTube 🔎](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6DjYgpltVeo) (unverified)
- **Source:** **Optimo, NTS, 9 June 2026** (track 1 — they *opened* with it).
- **Note:** Mutant-disco oddity — a sultry spoken vocal over a rubbery bassline asking "am I normal?" (answer: no, gloriously). At a beach party it's the peak-time left turn that makes the coldwave block feel *sexy* rather than austere. Note the Tigersushi lineage — same family as the How To Kill The DJ mixes.

### 21. Autumns – Alex, Can You Take The Bass Out?
*You Are Now Listening To Autumns* — **Opal Tapes (OPAL189)**, 2020 · funky EBM / post-punk
- **Format:** digital £7 (**cassette sold out**).
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://autumnsdss.bandcamp.com/album/you-are-now-listening-to-autumns)
- **Source:** **Amelia Holt, NTS "diet clinic", 3 March 2025** (track 8).
- **Note:** Derry's Autumns — the label calls it "ferociously pissed off, funky EBM post-punk": an SH-101 arp wound drum-tight under snarled vocals. Precisely the "sharp electro / alt-techno" Amelia brings. Use it as the aggression spike between cleaner electro cuts; the relentless arp makes long blends easy.

### 22. Marco Passarani – Wake Up Shake It
*Digipack Vol. 1* — **Running Back Studiomaster**, 2025 · 303 techno
- **Format:** digital €7 (**12" sold out** at BC).
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://marcopassarani.bandcamp.com/album/digipack-vol-1) · Discogs 🔎 [release/33081885](https://www.discogs.com/release/33081885) (marketplace for the sold-out 12")
- **Source:** **Amelia Holt, NTS diet clinic, 3 March 2025** (track 9 — straight after Autumns).
- **Note:** Rome techno royalty on Gerd Janson's Running Back sub-label: 303 squelch, thumping drums, vocal cuts — big-room-legible but weird enough for the trainspotters. The 4am hands-up moment. Amelia sequenced Autumns→Passarani herself; steal the pairing wholesale.

### 23. Bufiman – Rave The Forest
*Albumsi* — **Dekmantel (DKMNTL074)**, 2020 · tribal rave
- **Format:** digital €9; vinyl via Dekmantel.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://bufiman.bandcamp.com/album/albumsi) · [Dekmantel SoundCloud 🔎](https://soundcloud.com/dkmntl/dkmntl074-bufiman)
- **Source:** *Albumsi* LP, Dekmantel, 2020. 5:53.
- **Note:** The title says it — Bufiman's tribal-drums language pushed to rave pressure, the one moment his cosmic forest actually peaks. It lets *this* cluster hit peak-time without borrowing anyone else's records, and drops into "Pantasy" (13:22, same LP) if you want a psychedelic descent after.

### 24. Andy Rantzen – Will I Dream?
*Oz Waves* comp — **Efficient Space (ES004)**, 2017 (orig. 80s cassette) · industrial-dub
- **Format:** digital $16 AUD; **LP sold out**.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅](https://efficientspace.bandcamp.com/album/oz-waves) · SoundCloud 🔎 (label) · Discogs 🔎 [search "Oz Waves Efficient Space"](https://www.discogs.com/search/?q=Oz+Waves+Efficient+Space&type=release)
- **Source:** **NTS Party Socks w/ Minus Kendal & Rosie Ama, 9 Dec 2018** (track 5). 4:33.
- **Note:** Australian 80s industrial-dub (Itch-E & Scratch-E precursor), off Steele Bonus's DIY-cassette excavation — samples the HAL reactivation scene from *2010*. Hypnotic, paranoid, mid-tempo: the record that tips a chugging floor into genuinely strange territory. Official reissue, no bootleg guilt.

### 25. Barker – Look How Hard I've Tried
*Debiasing* EP — **Ostgut Ton**, 2018 · kickless techno
- **Format:** vinyl 12" + digital + streaming.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp 🔎](https://sambarker.bandcamp.com/album/debiasing) · Discogs 🔎 [master/1375059](https://www.discogs.com/master/1375059) · Boomkat 🔎 · streaming widely
- **Source:** **NTS Party Socks w/ Rosie Ama, 9 Dec 2018** (track 21).
- **Note:** Berghain techno with the kick surgically removed — pure synth-stab propulsion. On a beach at 3am it reads euphoric, not austere, and with no kick it layers beautifully over a percussion tool underneath. The one non-obscurity here, and it earns its slot as the peak's emotional crest.

## 🌗 PHASE 5 — THE TURN (drop the temperature, keep them moving) 

### 26. Snowy Red – Deep Desire
s/t — 1981 (reissues via **Onderstroom**) · Belgian coldwave
- **Format:** **vinyl-only** original (rare Belgian private press); *Ultimate Edition 1980–84* 5LP box (Onderstroom, 2011).
- **Hear / buy:** Discogs 🔎 [release/246394](https://www.discogs.com/release/246394) · [master/211341](https://www.discogs.com/master/211341) · box [release/2826499](https://www.discogs.com/release/2826499)
- **Source:** **Optimo, NTS, 12 May 2026** (track 13). *(Reissues are Onderstroom, not STROOM — Belgian coldwave royalty, Micky Mike.)*
- **Note:** Ice-cold minimal wave — drum machine, mournful synth, deadpan desire. The exact node where Optimo's post-punk instinct meets the Belgian STROOM universe, which makes it the conceptual centre of this set. Play it *after* the EBM peak as the first hint of comedown: bodies still moving, temperature dropping.

### 27. Nummer – 303 Of Life
*Daybreaker / 303 Of Life* — **Nummer Music** (Berlin), 2018 · acid-tribal deep house
- **Format:** vinyl 12" **€9 in stock** (ships ~5 days) + digital €3.
- **Hear / buy:** [Bandcamp ✅ (in stock)](https://nummermusic.bandcamp.com/album/daybreaker-303-of-life) · Discogs 🔎 [release/12668454](https://www.discogs.com/release/12668454)
- **Source:** **Amelia Holt, NTS diet clinic, 3 March 2025** (track 3).

## Getting these into the crate (→ `pg record` / `pg traktor`)

The house workflow — [`pg record`](recording) — is a **Spotify** process-tap: it captures a Spotify *playlist* into named, tagged FLACs, then [`pg traktor`](recording#traktor--pg-traktor-grid-snapped-cues) snaps hot cues to Traktor's own grid. That only reaches the records that are actually **on Spotify** — i.e. the tier (c) reference list. This crate is vinyl/Bandcamp-first *by design*, so the library work splits three ways:

1. **Streamable subset → `pg record` on the mini.** Build a Spotify playlist of the tier (c) tracks (and any tier (a)/(b) that happen to be on Spotify), then `uv run pg record <url> --skip-existing` on the always-on Mac mini (where the Spotify tap `.app` + Audio-Recording permission live, and where the FLAC library syncs via Syncthing). Then `pg traktor` per file.
2. **Bandcamp / digital purchases → buy the file, drop into the library, `pg ingest`.** The MFM/STROOM/Dekmantel/Efficient Space digital buys (tier a) are lossless-container FLAC/WAV straight from source — better than a Spotify tap. Ingest them directly; no capture needed.
3. **Vinyl-only (Snowy Red, Nini Raviolette, originals) → DVS capture** through the Xone:96 record route, or leave as "hear on the radio show" (tier b) until the record turns up.

> The `pg record` path **cannot** capture most of this guide — don't point it at a playlist and expect the whole set. It's the reference-tier tool; the soul of the crate is bought or dug.
